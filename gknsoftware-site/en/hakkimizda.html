About us

A small team, and you speak to us directly

What sets us apart from large software houses: you talk to the person doing the work. No project manager, no call centre, no ticketing system in between.

GOKHAN SOFTWARE builds software that makes an organisation's daily work easier. We work in insurance, legal and corporate IT.

In these sectors we know how data has to be protected and what stages a case file goes through — and we build the software accordingly. Alongside development we also cover licence, hardware and support needs.

We choose to stay small. Working with a handful of clients and knowing each one well produces better results than serving many superficially.
