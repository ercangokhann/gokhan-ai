Recourse Tracking System

Recourse files for 42 insurance companies · law firm and external lawyer network · end-to-end collection management
In production
360,000+records migrated, zero loss
42insurance companies
4channels: e-mail, WhatsApp, phone, documents
5-step mandatory workflow

The problem it solves

Recourse files were scattered across two legacy systems built over the years (MySQL and MSSQL) and Excel sheets. Files for 42 insurers, lawyer expenses, debtor correspondence and collections were tracked in separate places; incoming e-mails and messages were read and entered by hand. Which stage a file was at, which document was missing and what had been collected this month had to be assembled manually every time.

An enterprise platform that manages recourse (subrogation) files on behalf of insurers end to end — from opening to legal proceedings and litigation, from collection to closing. More than 360,000 records from two legacy systems were migrated with zero loss into one modern application, with both systems kept in sync during the transition. Incoming e-mails are read automatically and linked to the right file; routine correspondence is drafted and sent automatically; WhatsApp Business and PBX integration put the debtor one click away. Local AI and OCR read and classify documents; outgoing data is masked and every message to a debtor is sent only after staff approval. The in-house legal team and the external lawyer network work in the same system, which runs on on-premise servers with disk encryption and role-based access.

DDS Platin — Data Loss Prevention (DLP)

Endpoint monitoring and DLP system built in-house · central server, Windows agent, management console
In production
Liveactive monitoring across the organisation
Unlimitedcomputers and users
Bulkcentral rollout to every computer
GDPR/KVKKlogs and evidence

The problem it solves

Nobody knew where company data was going. If an employee copied a customer list to a USB stick, e-mailed it to a personal address or uploaded it to a cloud folder, no one noticed. Off-the-shelf DLP products were expensive with per-user licensing and did not fit the organisation's own rules — and a KVKK audit requires a record of these movements.

Not the installation of someone else's product: a three-layer data security system we wrote from scratch. The Windows agent on every computer logs application and window activity and blocks or reports data leaving via USB, e-mail and the cloud according to policy. Records are collected on the central server; the web and desktop management console is open only to two authorised people. The agent is rolled out to all computers in one go, runs alongside existing antivirus policies and carries no per-user or per-device licence cost.

Insurance claims, total-loss auctions and vehicle valuation · Claims Cockpit and Total-Loss Auction Tracking
In production
10integrated modules (Claims Cockpit)
Livein active daily use by the teams

The problem it solves

In major-loss and total-loss files, notifications, expert assessments, auction bids, market comparables and correspondence with vehicle owners were spread across different screens, spreadsheets and phones. A vehicle's market value and total-loss calculation were redone for every file, and a file's stage could only be learned by asking.

A two-part solution. Claims Cockpit brings the whole process — from first notification to management reporting — onto one screen according to industry standards: all main lines of business, approval authority limits, SBM/TRAMER, expert assignment, arbitration and total-loss/salvage/outstanding processes; it has 10 integrated modules and was presented to the board. Total-Loss Auction Tracking manages the auction of heavily damaged vehicles from registration to sale; it is in active use by the teams, and its embedded vehicle valuation module calculates market value and total loss on the same screen.

OVERWATCH — Security Audit Platform

Regular audit of the infrastructure from inside and outside
In production
Full scopeservers, firewall, APs, web
Inside + outsidetwo views, comparative report
Diffchanges since the previous scan
Authorisedwritten-permission targets only

The problem it solves

Organisations usually learn about security gaps after an incident. What is exposed on servers, the firewall and the wireless network is not checked regularly, and the difference between what is visible from the internal network and from the internet is unknown.

An audit platform with its own web console that scans servers, the firewall, wireless access points and web applications from both the internal network and the internet and compares the two views. Findings are verified with Greenbone/OpenVAS and turned into a prioritised, readable report. Only targets with written authorisation are scanned; no attacks or password cracking are performed. Where proof of exploitation is needed, we work with an independent penetration testing firm. The platform is currently being extended to bring endpoint monitoring and event logging, remote management, backup tracking and phishing awareness training into one console, so each client's devices, firewall and access points can be followed from the same screen.
